Findable isn't the same as chosen.

AI does check the big directories, but mostly to see who's in your city, not to decide who's good. What moves you onto the short list is what it can find about you specifically, and for most people that isn't much.

A brokerage template site or a lender page built to collect applications says the same things as thousands of others. That gives AI no reason to pick you over anyone else.

Rate sheets make you a transaction. A point of view makes you a recommendation.

AI sends you the business it thinks you're known for. So the goal isn't to be more visible in general. It's to be clearly known for the right thing.

Questions worth asking first

Most of my business comes from referrals. Why does this matter?

Referrals are the strongest business there is, and nothing here replaces them. But a lot of people who get your name now check AI before they call. If it knows nothing about you, or knows the wrong things, that referral can quietly go to someone else, and you never find out.

AI visibility gives your referrals somewhere good to land, so what they find confirms the recommendation instead of raising a question.

Can't I just update my brokerage or company website?

Keep it current, but a template site says what thousands of other agent and lender sites say, so it gives AI no reason to pick you. In one study of AI citations across 35 cities, the largest brokerage in the country finished eighth, behind a brokerage that only operates in Milwaukee. A big brand name isn't what gets you named. Being clearly known for something is.

Are you guaranteeing I'll show up in ChatGPT?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. The AI platforms rarely even agree with each other. In that same study, 82.6% of the agents named were cited by only one platform. What we guarantee is our side of the work, in writing, plus a before and after measurement so you can see exactly what moved.

How much of my time does this take?

Less than you'd think, but not zero. A few conversations up front so the work actually sounds like you, then reviewing pieces as they come back. You don't write, build, or post anything yourself unless you want to.
